## CA-ON-01 · The right person for the hearing

**Ontario · Ontario Landlord and Tenant Board · Needs practitioner review**

Toronto office · a second-floor practice near a busy streetcar route

A Toronto tenant has instructed Priya, an Ontario paralegal in good standing, to represent them at the LTB. The file is within Priya’s authorized scope; she is competent, prepared and available. An associate says only a lawyer can speak at the hearing. Which response is best?

A. Assign a lawyer because all tribunal advocacy is reserved to lawyers.
B. Proceed with Priya as the instructed representative, subject to the LTB’s requirements.
C. Let any experienced assistant appear under Priya’s licence.
D. Ask the client to waive the licensing requirements.

**Proposed answer: B.** Decisive fact: Priya is a licensed, eligible paralegal, and the other assignment checks are expressly satisfied.

- **A:** The premise treats all LTB representation as reserved to lawyers.
- **B:** Priya’s status and the stipulated scope, preparation and instructions support this assignment.
- **C:** A colleague cannot lend a licence to the assistant.
- **D:** Client preference cannot create professional authorization.

- **Who may represent an LTB party?:** Licensed Ontario lawyers and paralegals in good standing, and qualifying exempt representatives, may represent a party. A suspended licence does not permit representation. Eligibility does not establish competence, client instructions, availability, or success. Specific exemptions and any applicable statutory priority require review.
  - [Tribunals Ontario — Representation before the LTB](https://tribunalsontario.ca/documents/ltb/Practice%20Directions/Practice%20Direction%20on%20Representation%20before%20the%20LTB.html), Representatives; Licensed Representative; Unlicensed Representatives

**Practitioner check:** Confirm scope assumptions and distinguish eligibility from competence.
