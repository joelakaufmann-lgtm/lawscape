# How to play LawScape

[Play now](https://joelakaufmann-lgtm.github.io/lawscape/) · [Jurisdictions](JURISDICTIONS.md)

## Start with one activity

Create a new attorney and enter Hardsell & Firestone. Customize freely; you can
change your appearance later in **Journal → Wardrobe**.

- **A quick ethics question:** open **BarMail** at your office computer, select
  a practice pack, and open a subject line. Read all the facts before choosing
  your reply. Feedback explains the preferred answer and cites its rule.
- **A guided firm day:** open **Journal**. Read the synthetic source files,
  flag supporting passages, and work through the six writing assignments.
  Review both files and receive all six partner replies to unlock the capstone.

The inbox groups questions by your current experience: foundation, practice and
advanced. Completing questions raises that level across packs. A new study round
lets you revisit the current tier once its questions have been answered.

## Choose a jurisdiction

Use **Journal → Firm jurisdictions** for counts and a **Play** button, or select
a pack inside BarMail. California, New York, US MPRE-style, England & Wales
SQE-style and LawScape original dilemmas are available. Mixed practice includes
all five, so check each email's jurisdiction. The [jurisdiction catalog](JURISDICTIONS.md)
explains coverage and source-review limits.

Without a Work Phone, a catalog Play button takes you to the office computer.
With the upgrade, you can open BarMail in any location.

## Move and explore

Click or tap a floor tile to walk. Select a highlighted person or object to walk
over and interact. WASD and arrow keys also move. Use **Travel** for destinations
and doors for connected rooms. Close a panel with its close button or Escape.

Explore your office, Jim's corner office, Linda's office, the conference room,
courtroom, The Sidebar and your apartment. The courtroom has scripted characters;
live hearings and AI judges are planned. The Sidebar offers seating, drinks,
local table notes and a local completed-run billable-time board. Shared chat and
other-player presence are not available yet.

## Gold, Ethics and your character run

Correct ethics answers earn gold. Consecutive mistakes cause 30, then 45, then
60 Ethics damage; the Paralegal upgrade halves that damage. A correct streak
starts healing after two answers. Rest in your apartment and use available food
and coffee interactions to recover. Office and apartment upgrades affect play;
wardrobe accessories make your earnings visible.

**Zero Ethics ends the run.** Disbarment clears that character's gold, upgrades,
writing drafts and progress. A local completed-run billable-time record can
remain on the board. It is a study score, not a verified online ranking.

Sidebar alcoholic drinks slow movement for 40 seconds without Ethics damage.
Jim's office drinks have different consequences, explained when you interact.

## Writing, files and feedback

The apprenticeship uses fictional State of Juris policies and synthetic clients.
Open the record before drafting. Choose an issue, action and evidence reference,
then commit your reply. Commitment locks that attempt until an authored partner
reply arrives after a short delay. Rewards arrive once with the reply; a limited
revision may be offered.

Checklist choices earn study gold without Ethics damage. Written prose and file
notes are saved for self-comparison; no live AI grades them. File reviews reward
supported flags, and unsupported flags reduce the bonus. Read the partner's
feedback to see the relevant passages.

The Ethics Treatise Shelf upgrade opens the bundled Nevada, Arizona and California
reference library. These reference snapshots have their own dates and are not
a substitute for checking current law. Riley's paid hint identifies the rule for
the open ethics question when the required upgrades are owned.

## Saves and help

Progress saves automatically in this browser. Continue returns to that save.
There is no account or cross-device sync. Private browsing, clearing storage or
using a different hostname can leave you without the previous save. A local-file
save does not automatically move to the GitHub site.

Use **BarMail → Email HR · Report a bug** to prepare a report. Review and submit
it yourself on GitHub; the game sends nothing automatically. Avoid client facts
or private documents. You can also [report an issue directly](https://github.com/joelakaufmann-lgtm/lawscape/issues).

LawScape is unofficial educational practice, not legal advice or official exam
content. The rules and their exceptions differ by jurisdiction.
