How to play LawScape

Play now · Jurisdictions

Start with one activity

Create a new attorney and enter Hardsell & Firestone. Customize freely, or press Surprise me for a random look; you can change your appearance later in Journal → Wardrobe.

  • A quick ethics question: open BarMail at your office computer, select a practice pack, and open a subject line. Read all the facts before choosing your reply. Feedback explains the preferred answer and cites its rule.
  • A guided firm day: open Journal. Read the synthetic source files, flag supporting passages, and work through the six writing assignments. Review both files and receive all six partner replies to unlock the capstone.

The inbox groups questions by your current experience: foundation, practice and advanced. Completing questions raises that level across packs. A new study round lets you revisit the current tier once its questions have been answered.

Choose a jurisdiction

New attorneys start on the LawScape original dilemmas. Use Journal → Firm jurisdictions for counts and a Play button, or select a pack inside BarMail. California, New York, US MPRE-style, England & Wales SQE-style, Canada and the original dilemmas are available. Mixed practice includes all six, so check each email's jurisdiction. The jurisdiction catalog explains coverage and source-review limits.

Without a Work Phone, a catalog Play button takes you to the office computer. With the upgrade, you can open BarMail in any location.

The Canada pack's 25 questions pay gold and cost Ethics like every other pack; practitioner review of its answer key is still pending and the inbox says so. The pack also carries five email assignments. Choose All mail or Writing assignments to open them, then draft, commit, compare the authored guidance, and export your responses from a committed email. They are not graded. Open the practitioner review package.

Move and explore

Click or tap a floor tile to walk. Select a highlighted person or object to walk over and interact. WASD and arrow keys also move. Every building door opens onto Juris Avenue, a street with the law office, the apartments, the courthouse and The Sidebar; walk along it and step into a doorway. Travel (or T) is a quick-travel shortcut. Close a panel with its close button or Escape.

Explore your office, Jim's corner office, Linda's office, the conference room, the Associates' Floor, the courtroom, The Sidebar, the street and your apartment. The courtroom has scripted characters; live hearings and AI judges are planned. The Sidebar offers seating, drinks, local table notes, a local completed-run billable-time board and the Briefcase Dash arcade cabinet: insert 5 gold for three rounds, then press Space to jump caterpillars and the down arrow to duck butterflies on the walk to the office. Drinks at The Sidebar cost no Ethics but slow you down; arriving at the firm or the courthouse while still impaired costs 10 Ethics, once per drink. Your apartment, the street and the lounge are safe, and the office Espresso Machine upgrade sobers you up.

Gold, Ethics and your character run

Correct ethics answers earn gold, and a streak of correct answers multiplies it (×1.1, ×1.2 … up to ×1.5 from the sixth in a row). Consecutive mistakes cause 30, then 45, then 60 Ethics damage; the Paralegal upgrade halves that damage. A correct streak starts healing after two answers. Rest in your apartment and use available food and coffee interactions to recover. Office and apartment upgrades affect play; wardrobe accessories, jewelry and formal wear make your earnings visible. Five correct England & Wales answers earn a powdered barrister's wig; the tuxedo and evening gown need the apartment Wardrobe Rack. With 2,000 gold you can adopt a cat or a dog from the apartment furniture catalog and name it.

Zero Ethics ends the run. Disbarment clears that character's gold, upgrades, writing drafts and progress. A local completed-run billable-time record can remain on the board. It is a study score, not a verified online ranking.

Sidebar alcoholic drinks slow movement for 40 seconds without Ethics damage. Jim's office drinks have different consequences, explained when you interact.

Writing, files and feedback

The apprenticeship uses fictional State of Juris policies and synthetic clients. Open the record before drafting. Choose an issue, action and evidence reference, then commit your reply. Commitment locks that attempt until an authored partner reply arrives after a short delay. Rewards arrive once with the reply; a limited revision may be offered.

Checklist choices earn study gold without Ethics damage. Written prose and file notes are saved for self-comparison; no live AI grades them. File reviews reward supported flags, and unsupported flags reduce the bonus. Read the partner's feedback to see the relevant passages.

The Ethics Treatise Shelf upgrade opens the bundled Nevada, Arizona and California reference library. These reference snapshots have their own dates and are not a substitute for checking current law. Riley's paid hint identifies the rule for the open ethics question when the required upgrades are owned.

Saves and help

Progress saves automatically in this browser. Continue returns to that save. There is no account or cross-device sync. Private browsing, clearing storage or using a different hostname can leave you without the previous save. A local-file save does not automatically move to the GitHub site.

Playing online

The firm also runs an invite-only online build on Cloudflare. When you open that site, the title screen shows Play online · invite only. Signing in sends a one-time code to your invited email; nothing else is collected.

  • Your online attorney lives on the firm's server, separate from any local save. The first time you join, the creator can start from your local attorney's name and look, but never from local gold, Ethics or history.
  • Gold, Ethics, streaks, upgrades, writing assignments and billable time are settled by the server. If your connection drops mid-action, the action is retried or shown as failed; it is never applied twice.
  • Everyone signed in appears in the same rooms. Walk to the Associates' Floor from the main office or the travel menu, sit at an open desk to claim it, and open BarMail or the active files from the desk. The brass plate shows who holds each desk; a status tag above each attorney shows what they are doing, built from game events rather than typed text.
  • Who's in (the HUD button or the O key) lists everyone online, where they are, their desk and their status. The firm holds ten attorneys.
  • Signing in from a second tab or device moves your attorney there and ends the first session. If your sign-in expires, the game asks you to sign in again; nothing is lost.
  • Chat, the shared billable-hours board and labeled AI agents are not part of this pilot. Sidebar table notes stay private to your account.

Use BarMail → Email HR · Report a bug to prepare a report. Review and submit it yourself on GitHub; the game sends nothing automatically. Avoid client facts or private documents. You can also report an issue directly.

LawScape is unofficial educational practice, not legal advice or official exam content. The rules and their exceptions differ by jurisdiction.
