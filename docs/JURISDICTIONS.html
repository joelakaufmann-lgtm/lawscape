Firm assignments are a separate activity

The fictional State of Juris apprenticeship offers six writing assignments, two synthetic files and one capstone. It uses supplied office policies and authored checklist feedback. These activities are not included in the 171-question total and do not imply a reviewed real-jurisdiction firm curriculum.

Full jurisdiction-specific firm curricula remain future work. Canada is planned, with province-specific source and reviewer requirements; it is not currently a selectable question pack. Use the request form below the in-game catalog to save a local proposal, then optionally review and submit it on GitHub.

All entries are future curriculum targets. The regional headings are those in the supplied snapshot; an empty part of a region is not evidence that no member lives there. Countries and geographic locations do not map one-to-one to legal systems. In particular, United Kingdom does not mean one conduct regime; England & Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland require separate treatment. States, provinces, territories and specialist regulators may also need their own reviewed packs. A member's location does not establish bar admission.

Provenance and boundaries

This is an aggregate derivative of a user-supplied community document, not an independent verification of current membership or legal qualifications. The source describes incomplete profiles and approximate counts; the list should be expanded and corrected rather than treated as exhaustive. This public derivative omits member pseudonyms, biographical details, individual location records and chat excerpts. The original source is not distributed with the game.
