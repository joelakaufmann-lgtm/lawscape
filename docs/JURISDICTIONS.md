# A worldwide ethics curriculum

LawScape seeks to offer ethical questions from **every jurisdiction where there
is a LegalQuant**. This is a long-term curriculum goal, not current coverage.

## Playable now

| Material | Current scope |
| --- | --- |
| US MPRE-style | 69 original model-rule study questions; not an official NCBE set |
| England & Wales SQE-style | 28 original questions; not an official SQE set |
| State of Juris | Fictional firm policies, six writing assignments, two synthetic files and a capstone; the 21 legacy dilemmas retain mixed-source labels |
| Reference shelf | Bundled Nevada, Arizona and California material; not approved firm packs |

## Community-informed expansion targets

The supplied `legalquants-jurisdictions.md` was compiled **July 1, 2026**. Its
full list contains **27 geographic entries** across four regional headings:

| Region | Entries retained from the source |
| --- | --- |
| Americas | United States; Canada; Argentina; Uruguay |
| Europe | United Kingdom; Ireland; France; Germany; Netherlands; Belgium; Switzerland; Austria; Italy; Greece; Finland; Russia |
| Middle East & Africa | United Arab Emirates; Israel; Turkey |
| Asia-Pacific | Singapore; Hong Kong; China (mainland); India; Malaysia; Thailand; Australia; New Zealand |

All entries are future curriculum targets. The regional headings are those in
the supplied snapshot; an empty part of a region is not evidence that no member
lives there. Countries and geographic locations do not map one-to-one to legal
systems. In particular, United Kingdom does not mean one conduct regime;
England & Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland require separate treatment.
States, provinces, territories and specialist regulators may also need their
own reviewed packs. A member's location does not establish bar admission.

## Provenance and boundaries

This is an aggregate derivative of a user-supplied community document, not an
independent verification of current membership or legal qualifications. The
source describes incomplete profiles and approximate counts; the list should
be expanded and corrected rather than treated as exhaustive. This public
derivative omits member pseudonyms, biographical details, individual location
records and chat excerpts. The original source is not distributed with the game.

Source SHA-256: `638dc2d919dfd795362b80365f6259910768b5e3988899a1298de425611e28df`.

A new pack needs an exact jurisdiction and regulator, public primary sources
with dates, original questions, an identified reviewer and a reviewed answer
key/rubric. Cross-border comparisons must preserve each rule's scope and
exceptions. No contributor is committed or endorsed merely because their
jurisdiction appears here. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
