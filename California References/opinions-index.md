# California State Bar Formal Ethics Opinions — Topical Index

**Issuing body:** State Bar of California Standing Committee on Professional Responsibility and Conduct (COPRAC).

**Use:** Skim this index at the start of every question to identify potentially on-point opinions, then open `opinions-full.md` and read each on-point opinion in full before quoting or relying on it. Do not rely on the short topic tags as substitutes for holdings.

**Renumbering warning:** Opinions issued before 2018 cite the former ('1992') Rules of Professional Conduct (e.g., Rule 3-310, 5-100, 5-200, 3-600, 4-100, 2-100, 1-400, 3-700). When relying on a pre-2018 opinion, consult the cross-reference tables in `rpc.md` and verify that the reasoning survives under the current rule.

**Total opinions indexed:** 177 (spanning 1965–2024).

## Revision, Reliability, and Supersession Notes

- **Pre-2018 Rules.** All opinions dated **2017 and earlier** cite the former ("1992") California Rules of Professional Conduct. Rule numbers such as 1-100, 1-300, 1-320, 1-400, 1-600, 2-100, 2-200, 3-110, 3-210, 3-300, 3-310, 3-320, 3-400, 3-500, 3-600, 3-700, 4-100, 4-200, 4-210, 4-300, 4-400, 5-100, 5-110, 5-120, 5-200, 5-210, 5-220, 5-300, 5-310, 5-320, and 6-101 through 6-200 are **former** rule numbers. Use the cross-reference tables in `rpc.md` to locate the current rule.
- **Post-2018 Rules.** Opinions dated **2019-197** and later generally cite the current numbering (1.0 through 8.5). However, because opinion-drafting sometimes began before the November 1, 2018 effective date, verify the citations within each opinion.
- **Superseded or modified opinions.** Some older opinions have been expressly superseded or effectively superseded by later opinions or by the 2018 rules revision. When the topic tag in this index points to multiple opinions, prefer the latest opinion on the same point and check for express supersession language in the later opinion.
- **California Supreme Court approval.** COPRAC opinions are advisory; they are not approved or adopted by the California Supreme Court unless expressly incorporated by the Court in a published decision. Cite them as persuasive authority, not binding authority.
- **State Bar Act.** California also has the State Bar Act (Business and Professions Code §§ 6000 et seq.), which contains substantive ethics duties (e.g., § 6068(e) on confidentiality; § 6106 on moral turpitude; § 6125 on unauthorized practice; § 6211 on IOLTA). The Act's provisions operate concurrently with the Rules of Professional Conduct and are frequently cited alongside rule citations in opinions.

## How to Use This Index

1. Identify the **primary issue** in the ethics question (e.g., "former-client conflict + screening").
2. Find the matching **Topical Cross-Reference** section above.
3. Note the opinion numbers. Prefer the most recent opinion on the point as a first read.
4. Open `opinions-full.md`, locate the opinion (sections are named `CAL_YYYY-NNN`), and read the **full** text.
5. Check the **Authorities Interpreted** section for the precise rule cited. If a former rule number is cited, translate via `rpc.md`'s cross-reference.
6. When quoting the holding in a CREAC analysis, cite as `Cal. State Bar Formal Op. YYYY-NNN`.
