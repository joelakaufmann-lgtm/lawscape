# LawScape

**[Play LawScape in your browser](https://joelakaufmann-lgtm.github.io/lawscape/)** · [How to play](docs/PLAYING.md) · [Jurisdictions](docs/JURISDICTIONS.md)

Build a legal career at Hardsell & Firestone, where the partners need answers,
the printer needs attention, and HR has been temporarily unstaffed since 1987.
Create an attorney, answer workplace dilemmas, inspect case files, earn gold
and improve your office while protecting your Ethics.

![An attorney exploring the LawScape office](media/demo-30s/captures/office-full.png)

Free to play. No account, installation or API key required. The current release
is **0.9.0**, with six rule shelves (Nevada, Arizona, California, New York, the FLSC Model Code and the SRA Standards), a study-list export, a walkable street, a bigger wardrobe,
adoptable pets, and California, New York and Canada practice. Prefer a quick look first? [Watch the 30-second demo](media/lawscape-demo-30s.mp4).

## Your first five minutes

1. Choose **Create an Attorney**, customize your character and enter the firm.
2. Open **Journal** for a guided first assignment, or **BarMail** at your office
   computer for a quick ethics question.
3. Open a subject line, read the facts, choose your response and read the
   explanation. In writing assignments, review the supplied file before replying.
4. Use **Journal → Firm jurisdictions** to choose a question pack. New attorneys
   start on the original dilemmas; each pack opens directly in BarMail and
   **Mixed practice** draws from all six.
5. Walk out onto Juris Avenue to reach the courthouse, The Sidebar and your
   apartment (or use **Travel** as a shortcut), talk to the partners, and use
   **Journal → Wardrobe** to change your look. Gold buys accessories, jewelry,
   formal wear, upgrades and a pet.

Click or tap to walk and interact. WASD and arrow keys also move your attorney.
The Work Phone upgrade lets you use BarMail outside the office.

## Before you start

Progress saves in this browser, without cross-device sync. Clearing browser
storage loses the save. Wrong ethics answers damage Ethics; reaching zero ends
the character run and resets its gold, upgrades and drafts. Correct streaks and
rest help restore Ethics.

The fictional apprenticeship awards study gold without Ethics damage. Its
checklist choices receive authored feedback; your written prose is saved for
self-comparison, not graded by live AI. Sidebar notes are local.

An invite-only **online firm** runs on Cloudflare: invited players sign in with
an emailed code, keep their attorney on the server, and see each other walk,
claim open desks and work on the shared Associates' Floor. The GitHub Pages
copy and the file you open from disk stay single-player. See
[Playing online](docs/PLAYING.md#playing-online) and the
[operator runbook](docs/ONLINE.md). Shared chat and live courtroom hearings
are still future work.

LawScape is unofficial educational practice, not legal advice or official bar
exam content. Read the jurisdiction and source note on each question.
