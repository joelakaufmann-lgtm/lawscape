The operative authorities are the court-adopted rules. NYSBA's PDF republishes
those rules alongside commentary; the commentary is not adopted by the courts
and was not used as answer authority. The rule text, rather than comments, was
read for each tested provision. Current court orders and the amendment index
were used to check version changes. The older court-hosted 2017 compilation
was not treated as the current consolidated rules.

Direct downloads of court PDFs returned HTTP 403 in this run. Court pages and
available order text were reviewed through web retrieval; the November 2025
scanned order could not be independently text-compared. The current NYSBA
republication supplied readable rule text, including the amended Rules 5.5 and
8.3. Its download and derived text were hashed; see [source receipt](source-receipt.json).
This review does not certify the official NYCRR compilation or every intervening
interpretation. A New York practitioner review remains useful and is not implied
by the owner's publication instruction.

## Corrections before integration

- Escrow account answers now include Part 1300 compliance for an out-of-state
  bank and written approval specifying the branch name and address.
- The former-client scenario expressly includes a remaining lawyer's access
  to material protected information; the lateral-screening answer preserves
  Rule 1.7 waiver conditions.
- Prospective-client screening includes limited intake, internal notice,
  effective screening, no fee share, external notice and competent/diligent
  representation. The confidentiality postponement for notice is acknowledged.
- Withdrawal uses the July 2026 wording and distinguishes permission required
  by tribunal rules from a universal requirement in every representation.
- Part 1215 distinguishes the letter's limited delayed-delivery grounds from
  the separately stated signed-retainer alternative in section 1215.1(c).
- Living-expense assistance is tied to this contingent-fee engagement; a private
  lawyer providing free services is not categorically excluded from the rule's
  separate exception. The solicitation facts exclude the listed relationships.
- The nonrefundable-retainer facts now expressly concern payment for services,
  not a payment solely for availability.
- Rule 5.5's obsolete subsection citation was removed. Reporting exceptions
  now acknowledge Rules 1.6, 1.9 and 1.18.
- The short noncompete scenario no longer makes an unresolved bonus-forfeiture
  claim; it tests the stated employment restriction. Short explanations use
  rule-based paraphrases and clarify when an escrow fee is due.

## Maintaining this pack

Edit the numbered Markdown answer key or `short-scenarios.json`, then run
`npm run states:build`. Preserve identifiers. Recheck adopted rules and relevant
interpretive law when revising. Structural tests do not establish legal accuracy.
