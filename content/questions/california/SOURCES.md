# California question source review

**20 original questions · source check September 27, 2026 · unofficial educational practice.**

The project owner authorized implementation and publication. Codex drafted and
source-checked this pack; no independent California practitioner sign-off is
recorded. This is a question pack, not a fully reviewed California firm curriculum
or legal advice. Read the [questions and answer key](QUESTIONS.md).

## Authority and version

- [State Bar current rules index](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/legal-professionals/rules/rules-professional-conduct/current-rules-professional-conduct).
- [2026 Rules of Professional Conduct compilation linked by that index](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/sites/default/files/portals/0/documents/rules/Rules-of-Professional-Conduct.pdf).

The question answers are paraphrases of the adopted rule text, with rule and
paragraph citations in every scenario. The current index and its linked 2026
compilation were checked directly. Pending proposals were not treated as adopted
rules. The older bundled reference shelf was retained as a separate snapshot.

The compilation was downloaded from the publisher and converted using pdftotext.
The download date in UTC is September 28; the local review date is September 27.
[Source receipt](source-receipt.json) records the URL and SHA-256. Automated
version adapters do not cover these ethics rules; this was a direct index/text
review, not an automated proof that no later authority exists.

## Scope checks

The 20 items cover written settlement offers; uninsured-lawyer disclosure;
flat fees and true retainers; fee divisions; current and former clients; business
transactions; substantial gifts; third-party payment; advance fees and disputed
funds; returning files; adverse authority; preservation; threats; represented
people; inadvertent privileged material; supervisors; confidentiality; and
reporting misconduct.

California-specific safeguards are preserved: informed written consent, the
substantial-gift independent-review exception, signed flat-fee disclosures above
$1,000, both stopping unnecessary review and notifying the sender, and the narrow
criminal-act confidentiality exception. Each question supplies facts needed to
exclude the competing exception it tests. These are original hypotheticals, not
California Bar examination questions.

## Maintaining this pack

Edit `scenarios.json`, rerun `npm run states:build`, and review both the generated
answer key and feedback. Check current sources again when changing the law or
facts. A software test establishes structure and behavior, not legal correctness.
