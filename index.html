How to play

Click or tap the floor to walk. Select people and office objects to interact. Open BarMail, choose the ethical response, earn gold, and upgrade your office and apartment.

Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys move. B opens BarMail, R opens your record, T opens travel, and Escape closes a window.

LawScape is a fictional educational game. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Rules cited (Arizona ERs, Nevada NRPC) are simplified for gameplay — always read the operative text.
