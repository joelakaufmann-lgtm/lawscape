How to play

Click or tap the floor to walk. Select people and office objects to interact. Open BarMail, choose the ethical response, earn gold, and upgrade your office and apartment.

Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys move. B opens BarMail, R opens your record, T opens travel, and Escape closes a window.

Your first firm day starts with a file, an impatient partner, and a draft that is suspiciously confident. Inspect the evidence, commit your reply, earn something worth wearing. Explore seven rooms, meet Derek Balam in court, and take a break at The Sidebar.

LawScape is a fictional educational game. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. US and England-and-Wales rules are simplified for gameplay — always read the operative text.
