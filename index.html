The stakes are part of the game

Build your practice.
Try not to lose it.

Wrong answers can cost Ethics. At zero, your attorney is disbarred and the run ends. Your gold, upgrades, drafts, and apprenticeship progress are lost.

That apartment? That office? You start again from nothing.

This is LawScape’s game mechanic. The separate fictional writing and evidence exercises award study gold without Ethics damage.
