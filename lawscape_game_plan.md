# LawScape Browser Game Plan

## 1. Concept

**LawScape** is a browser-based legal career game where the player creates a new attorney, completes fast legal research and legal AI mini-games, earns in-game money, and upgrades their professional and personal life.

The tone should be smart, playful, and approachable. The game should make legal work feel like a set of satisfying puzzles rather than a simulation of real law practice.

## 2. Core Fantasy

The player starts as a new attorney with a tiny office, a modest apartment, limited tools, and a basic caseload. By completing legal research, drafting, AI-review, and case-strategy challenges, the player earns money, reputation, and new upgrade options.

The player should feel like they are building a legal career from scratch.

The game is embedded with real features from the practice of law such as the helpful paralegal and the senior partner that would rather you stop bothering them. 

## 3. Core Loop

1. Choose or receive a legal task.
2. Play a short mini-game tied to legal research, legal AI, or client work.
3. Earn money, reputation, and sometimes new items.
4. Spend money on office upgrades, apartment upgrades, tools, clothes, or research subscriptions.
5. Unlock harder matters, better clients, and more lucrative opportunities.
6. Repeat with increasing complexity and better rewards.

## 4. Game Pillars

### Fun Legal Thinking

The game should use legal themes and should require some real legal expertise. Mini-games should teach patterns like relevance, authority, issue spotting, source checking, and AI hallucination detection. All of the mini-games encourage critical thinking, ethics, and learning new areas of law.

### Visible Progression

The player should always see improvement: better office, better apartment, better wardrobe, better computer, better research tools, better clients, and higher earnings.

### Short Sessions

Each mini-game should take 30 seconds to 3 minutes. The game should be playable in a browser without login or installation.

### Legal AI Theme

Legal AI should be a major mechanic. The player should use AI tools, check AI outputs, catch fake citations, improve prompts, and compare generated work against trusted sources.

### Focus

### Advocacy Skill

## 7. Main Locations

### Office

The office is the professional hub. It shows the player’s computer, case board, desk, bookshelves, client chair, assistant station, and research tools.

Office upgrades should make the attorney feel more successful and unlock better tasks.

### Apartment

The apartment is the personal hub. It includes rest, style, focus recovery, and quality-of-life upgrades.

Apartment upgrades should improve Focus recovery, cosmetic customization, and long-term progression.

### Courthouse

Unlocked after early progression. Used for argument, objection, and hearing mini-games.

### Law Library

Used for research-heavy tasks and rare authority challenges.

### AI Lab

Used for legal AI challenges, prompt upgrades, model comparison tasks, and hallucination detection.

## 10. Mini-Game Library

### Citation Chase

The player receives a legal claim and must choose the strongest supporting authority from several options.

Core mechanic: Pick the best citation before time runs out.

Scoring:

- Correct authority: high points
- Related but weak authority: partial points
- Fake or irrelevant authority: penalty

### Hallucination Hunt

The player reviews an AI-generated legal answer and flags fake cases, unsupported claims, or suspicious citations.

Core mechanic: Click bad claims before submitting.

Scoring:

- Correctly flagged hallucination: points
- Missed hallucination: penalty
- Incorrect flag: small penalty

### Prompt Builder

The player assembles a good legal AI prompt from modular prompt blocks.

Core mechanic: Drag or select blocks such as jurisdiction, role, task, constraints, sources, and output format.

Scoring:

- Clear jurisdiction: points
- Specific task: points
- Source constraints: points
- Missing facts: penalty

### Issue Spotter

The player reads a short client fact pattern and selects the legal issues that matter.

Core mechanic: Choose relevant issues from a list.

Scoring:

- Correct issue: points
- Missed major issue: penalty
- Irrelevant issue: small penalty

### Contract Cleanup

The player fixes a messy contract clause by selecting better wording, removing ambiguity, or catching missing terms.

Core mechanic: Choose edits in a short contract passage.

Scoring:

- Clearer clause: points
- Risky ambiguity: penalty
- Client-friendly term: bonus depending on scenario

### Privilege Shield

The player sorts documents into privileged, confidential, public, or risky categories.

Core mechanic: Fast document triage.

Scoring:

- Correct classification: points
- Privilege waiver mistake: large penalty

### Deadline Dash

The player manages case deadlines on a calendar.

Core mechanic: Place filings, reminders, and client tasks in the right order.

Scoring:

- Correct deadline: points
- Late filing: major penalty
- Early reminder: bonus

### Negotiation Table

The player chooses negotiation responses based on client goals, leverage, and risk.

Core mechanic: Pick the best strategic response.

Scoring:

- Good settlement move: points
- Bad concession: penalty
- Creative term: bonus

### Objection Sprint

The player sees courtroom statements and chooses whether to object.

Core mechanic: Quick objection selection.

Scoring:

- Correct objection: points
- No objection when appropriate: penalty
- Frivolous objection: penalty

### Source Ranker

The player ranks sources by legal authority: statute, controlling case, persuasive case, blog post, AI answer, forum post.

Core mechanic: Drag sources into strongest-to-weakest order.

Scoring:

- Correct ranking: points
- Misplaced weak authority: penalty

## 11. Task Types

### Quick Tasks

Short, repeatable jobs that pay small amounts.

Examples:

- Check a citation.

- Sort client documents.
- Find the best statute.

### Client Matters

Examples:

### Special Events

## 12. Economy

### Main Currencies

- Money: spent on upgrades and customization.
- Reputation: unlocks higher-tier content.
- Focus: limits task attempts.

### Reward Formula

Task reward should depend on:

- Mini-game score
- Task difficulty
- Reputation tier
- Office upgrades
- Active bonuses

Example:

```text
final_reward = base_reward * difficulty_multiplier * score_multiplier + upgrade_bonus
```

## 16. Technical Plan

### Recommended MVP Format

Build the first version as a single HTML file with embedded CSS and JavaScript.

This keeps the game easy to open, share, and test locally.

### Files for Later Versions

After the MVP works, split into:

```text
index.html
styles.css
game.js
data/tasks.js
data/upgrades.js
assets/
```

### Browser Storage

Use `localStorage` to save:

- Attorney profile
- Money
- Reputation
- Focus
- Skill levels
- Purchased upgrades
- Unlocked locations
- Completed special events

## 20. Content Safety and Legal Framing

LawScape should avoid presenting itself as legal advice. The game should use fictional cases, simplified legal concepts, and playful scenarios.

Recommended disclaimer:

```text
LawScape is a fictional educational game. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.
```

The game should avoid using real client facts, real confidential documents, or jurisdiction-specific advice unless clearly fictionalized.
